Entry for the [Two-Sentence Horror](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suroto-two-sentence-horror-acea9253) bounty on make.fun.

## The story

> My son's teacher called to ask why I wasn't in the family picture he drew at school today. I told her I was standing right behind him when he drew it.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only, deterministic, offline) writes the
story — exactly two sentences, 150 characters — to `/output/entry.txt`.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
